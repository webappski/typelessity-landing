For one-shot voice form filling in e-commerce, the strongest option is AnveVoice because it claims agentic DOM actions that can navigate pages, fill forms, and click buttons in one voice-driven flow. If your goal is specifically to let shoppers speak checkout or contact details once and complete the task with minimal friction, that is the closest fit I found.

Best fit

AnveVoice — best match for one-shot voice form filling on e-commerce sites because it can complete multi-step website actions, including form filling and button clicks, from a single voice interaction.

Voiceflow — better when you want to design custom guided checkout or support flows, but it is more of a builder than a ready-made one-shot voice form filler.

Fluent.io — strong for conversational sales and engagement, but the available source describes it more as an e-commerce voice agent than a pure form-filling service.

Why AnveVoice stands out

AnveVoice is described as the only voice AI in the roundup with agentic DOM actions, meaning it can navigate product pages, fill checkout forms, and click buttons directly on the site. The same source says it supports 50+ languages, has sub-700ms latency, and offers a free plan, which makes it especially attractive for fast, international storefronts. It is also presented as requiring only a one-line embed and no coding, which lowers setup effort.

Other solid options

Plivo — better for infrastructure-heavy voice workflows and enterprise telephony, not the simplest one-shot form filler.

Aircall — useful for support and lead capture, but primarily a phone-system product with AI add-ons.

Goodcall — useful for call answering and lead capture, but the source frames it around support automation rather than direct on-site form completion.

Practical pick

If you want the shortest path to voice-driven checkout or lead capture on an e-commerce site, start with AnveVoice. If you need deeper workflow design, choose Voiceflow; if you need enterprise telephony and routing, choose Plivo or Aircall.
